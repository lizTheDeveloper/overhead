overhead: listen for drones

Leave this page open and your phone listens for the hum of drone propellers. When it hears one, it puts a dot on the map.

Audio never leaves your phone. It's analysed here, in this page, a second at a time, and thrown away. Only "possible drone, this time, this confidence" is sent, with your location rounded to ~300 m.

It makes mistakes. Long held notes in music, some engines and fans can sound like a drone to it, and far or small drones get missed. On the map these dots are marked as automatic. Several phones hearing the same thing is what counts.

Best results: outside or by an open window, away from music and traffic, phone plugged in, screen left on (this page keeps it awake while listening). Don't cover the microphone.
